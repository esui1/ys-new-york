The cloth

Merino carries the warmth.
Silk carries the light.

Wool and silk are dyed separately to the sealed lab dip, then plied. The cloth is woven double-face in Zürich, finished below 30°C, pressed flat — and never brushed. Every edge is turned 3 mm once and overcast by hand, seven to eight stitches to the centimetre.

The navy in her hands falls between Marine Sombre and Marine Clair — the whole design lives in that twelve-point gap.
